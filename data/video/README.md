# Video

Each subdirectory matches a topic in [text](../text). Clips are H.264 MP4 at 360p or 480p with a low bitrate, since EmbeddingGemma 2 samples video at 1 frame per second.
The suffix in each filename gives its resolution.

All clips except the gundam opening were re-encoded with the video capped at 400 kbps and audio at 64 kbps mono AAC.
